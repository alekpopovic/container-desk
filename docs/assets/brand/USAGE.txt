ContainerDesk — Brand kit

Your servers. Your SSH. One workspace.
A native desktop for Docker over SSH.

Use mark.svg for the shared app/web icon, the matching light/dark wordmark for the background, banner.svg for the README and social-card.png for link previews. PNG exports are provided at their original dimensions.

Preserve proportions and colors. Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the mark width. Normal navigation minimum: 24 px. Do not rotate, stretch or imply affiliation with Docker, Apple, GitHub or OpenSSH.
